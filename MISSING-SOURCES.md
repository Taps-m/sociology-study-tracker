Checked 6 Sep 2026. Two different questions, two very different answers.

## 1. No source in the standard textbooks — 5 chapters

The three textbooks in `standard books/` — Rao's *Principles of Sociology*,
Haralambos, and Sangwan's *Essential Sociology* — between them cover **81 of the
85 chapters**. These are the exceptions, from the app's own chapter-and-page map
in `src/data/standardBooks.ts`.

### Mentioned, but no chapter that covers it

| # | Chance | Asked | Group | Chapter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 74 | 13% | 1 of 14 | A only | P2 Major Social Groups — Linguistic and regional groups |

Five chapters, nine questions in fourteen years.

### Simmel is the one that matters

He ranks **18th of 85** — the top of the standard-yield group — and he is the
only major thinker in the syllabus with no textbook chapter behind him. WBCS
asked him in 2014 and again in 2020. The other four on this list are minor by
comparison; Simmel is not, and he will have to be sourced separately.

The other four are cheap to close: two are Indian policy topics (child welfare
programmes, drug addiction) that a government report or a current-affairs
compilation answers, and celebrity culture is a short read from any general
sociology text. Two of the four are **Group B only**, where three questions are
asked and you answer two, so there is no third option to escape to.

## 2. No source in the two notes PDFs — 15 chapters

A separate and larger gap, because both notes PDFs follow the **UPSC** syllabus.
Checked by searching their section headings, not their body text: a word in a
paragraph is not a section you can study from — "youth" occurs eleven times in
the Paper 2 notes and heads nothing.

**This corrects a looser claim I made earlier.** I said nine chapters and 11% of
the paper. Heading by heading it is fifteen chapters and **40 of the 224
questions, 18%**.

**The whole Science and Technology unit is missing from the notes.** All four
chapters. UPSC's Paper 1 has no such unit, so neither PDF carries a word of it —
"scientific temper", "social control of science" and "technology and social
change" return zero heading hits in both files. Ten questions in fourteen years,
three of the four Group B only.

**This gap is covered by the textbooks.** Only drug addiction and child welfare
appear on both lists. Everything else here has a chapter in Rao, Haralambos or
Sangwan — so for these fifteen, use the books rather than the notes, and expect
the notes to be silent rather than assuming you have missed a section.

## Reading the numbers

"Chance" is the pooled model's estimate for a given year and "#" its rank of 85,
both from the Question prediction screen. "Asked" is the raw count from the 224
questions in `pyq.ts`, 2010–2023. "Group" is where a chapter has actually
appeared — Group A, Group B, or both.

Section 1 is only as good as `standardBooks.ts`, which is a hand-built map and
has been wrong before: chapter 8 of the Paper I notes was recorded as starting on
page 312 when it opens on 294. Verify a page before trusting it.

*Nothing here is a reason to skip a chapter. A chapter with no source is a
chapter you have to find a source for.*
